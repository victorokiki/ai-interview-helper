Interview Helper: autofill profile

Your answers for job application forms. Stored only in this browser, never sent to the app or anywhere else.

    How to fill a form

    On an application form, click the Interview Helper button in the toolbar and press Fill this form, or press Alt+Shift+F (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts). The extension never submits a form, never clicks Next, never ticks consent boxes and never changes a field that already has an answer.
