## Payments

- Stripe cards: 2.9% + 30¢ per payment. International cards add 1.5%; currency conversion adds 1%.
- Stripe Billing: 0.7% of billed volume.
- Usage-based billing: you set up a Billing Meter, then send usage with `POST /v1/billing/meter_events`. For new usage-based integrations, Stripe now recommends Metronome ($0.04 per 1,000 events + 0.8%).
