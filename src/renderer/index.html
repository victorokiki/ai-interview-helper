                      Keep linked Markdown notes of your jobs, interviews, practice, career documents, lessons and question bank in your Obsidian vault (or any folder).                       Browser extension
                      
      
                      One extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave: it adds the job posting you're looking at to Jobs, and fills application forms from your autofill profile (Fill this form or Alt+Shift+F; it never submits). It reads or fills a page only when you click it, keeps your autofill profile in the browser, and can't read anything from this app. It talks to this app through the browser's native messaging: no network port is opened. Already installed? Press reload on its card in chrome://extensions to get the new version.
